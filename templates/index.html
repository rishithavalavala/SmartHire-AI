AI-powered recruitment assistant that combines resume screening, skill-gap analysis, AI interview generation, candidate scoring, and recruiter recommendations in one platform.

Why SmartHire AI?

  • Resume–Job Description match scoring
  • Matched skills and missing skills detection
  • AI-generated technical MCQ interview
  • Weak skill identification and improvement suggestions
  • Recruiter dashboard with final recommendation

Core Features

Resume Matching

Upload resume and compare it with a target job description to generate match score, matched skills, and missing skills.

AI Interview Engine

Generate role-based MCQ interview questions using resume content, job description, and identified skill gaps.

Recruiter Intelligence

View candidate interview score, weak areas, and final recommendation in a recruiter-friendly dashboard.

How It Works

1

Analyze Resume

Candidate uploads resume and job description. SmartHire AI extracts skills and calculates the resume match score.

2

Take AI Interview

The platform generates MCQ-based technical questions based on resume topics and missing skills.

3

Get Final Report

Candidate receives score and improvement plan, while recruiter gets a final hiring recommendation dashboard.
